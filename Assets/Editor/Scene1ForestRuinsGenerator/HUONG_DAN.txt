SCENE 1 - FOREST RUINS MAP
===========================

Unity version: 2022.3.62f3

HOW TO CREATE THE SCENE
1. Open this project in Unity.
2. Wait until scripts/assets finish importing.
3. On the top menu select:
   Tools > Goddess Trial > Generate Scene 1 - Forest Ruins
4. Unity creates:
   Assets/Scenes/Scene1_ForestRuins.unity

WHAT THE GENERATED SCENE CONTAINS
- Start zone with broken portal and PlayerSpawn marker.
- Beginner combat arena with 3 enemy spawn markers.
- Forest fork with optional reward branch.
- Second arena with rocks/pillars for cover and 5 enemy spawn markers.
- Boss arena made from ruins, gate, portal, walls and pillars.
- Mountains, trees and rocks framing the map.
- Preview camera and directional light.

IMPORTANT
- The original Level1 scene is not overwritten.
- Colored cylinders are gameplay markers only. Replace them with your current player,
  enemy spawner, boss and trigger systems.
- After adding your gameplay colliders/components, bake NavMesh again.
- If you run the generator again, Scene1_ForestRuins.unity is recreated.
