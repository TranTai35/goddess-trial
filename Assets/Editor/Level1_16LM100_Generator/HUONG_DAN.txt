LEVEL 1 - 16 LM100 BRANCH ARENAS

1. Copy Level1SixteenLM100Generator.cs into:
   Assets/Editor/

2. Return to Unity and wait until compilation finishes.

3. Run:
   Tools > Goddess Trial > Generate Level1 - 16 LM100 Branch Arenas

4. The generated scene is saved at:
   Assets/Scenes/Level1_16LM100_BranchArenas.unity

The generator creates exactly 16 LM100 objects at:
- Z = 400: X = 0, 100, 200, 300
- Z = 200: X = 0, 100, 200, 300
- Z = 100: X = 0, 100, 200, 300
- Z = 0:   X = 0, 100, 200, 300

Gameplay markers:
- PlayerSpawn
- Small_Encounter_4_Enemies
- Left_Arena_15_Enemies
- Right_Arena_15_Enemies
- LevelExit_UpperTemple

The row at Z=400 is separated from Z=200 by a water gap. Two long bridges at X=100 and X=200 connect the areas.

After adding Player, Enemy and gameplay scripts, bake NavMesh again.
